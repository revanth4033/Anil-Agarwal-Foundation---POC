Who we are

A vision rooted in inclusive growth.

Our purpose: to help people and communities access opportunities for a better tomorrow.

The Anil Agarwal Foundation is the philanthropic arm of the Vedanta Group. It has its own identity, its own leadership and its own mandate: to invest in people and communities, not in the interests of any business.

Anil Agarwal’s Giving Pledge

75%

Anil Agarwal’s Giving Pledge commitment

Anil Agarwal joined The Giving Pledge in 2021, reaffirming his 2014 commitment to devote 75% of his family’s wealth to charity. His philanthropic priorities focus on tackling poverty, improving children’s nutrition and education, and empowering women, particularly in India.
