## How to read this document

- This document records **content, structure, order, hierarchy and interactions only**. The wireframe's colours, fonts, imagery, card shapes and layout are **exploratory and not approved**. Visual design follows `AAF_BRAND_FOUNDATION.md`.
- **Copy is quoted exactly as it appears in the PDF**, including punctuation, curly quotes (“ ” ’), em dashes (—), middle dots (·), arrows (→ ↓ ▾), line-ending full stops and any spelling. Text inside `code formatting` is verbatim.
- **Casing:** small labels (eyebrows, tags, meta lines) are *displayed* in uppercase in the wireframe, but the PDF's underlying text is in the casing recorded here. Treat the uppercase display as a styling choice, not copy.
- **Placeholders** (`XX`, `[Name]`, `[Story title]` and so on) are preserved exactly and must not be filled with invented content.
- **Image notes:** the PDF labels every image with a description such as `Photo — [children at a Nand Ghar centre, midday light]`. These are **image briefs, not on-page copy**. They're recorded so the right photography can be sourced. The images in the wireframe are illustrative only and must not be used as AAF programme imagery.
- ⚠️ marks an item that needs review. All such items are collected in [Pending content and open questions](#pending-content-and-open-questions).

## Homepage overview

The homepage moves the visitor from **emotional entry** (hero film) to **identity and purpose** (who we are, the founder's pledge, the approach). It then covers **what AAF does** (programmes), **who it is for** (people), **evidence** (numbers, stories), **accountability** (leadership, reports, films, news) and finally **action** (collaboration), closing with the footer.

The six programme areas form the content backbone. They recur, with the same names, in the programme cards, the Stories filters, the Films tags and the footer's "Areas of Work" column.
